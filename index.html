About Just Jap Imports

With over 20 years of experience, we’re a family-run car sales company built on a genuine passion for cars. We specialise in quality vehicles and above all we have a real love for Japanese motoring. From iconic classics to modern performance cars, we’re enthusiasts first and dealers second. Whether it’s the legendary 1994 Toyota Supra or the timeless 1969 Toyota Celica GT, we appreciate the character, heritage and engineering that make these cars so special. Our aim is simple: to source and supply cars that we’d be proud to own ourselves, while offering our customers a friendly, honest and personal service.

Family run, Car enthusiasts through and through.
